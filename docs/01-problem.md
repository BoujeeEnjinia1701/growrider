# GrowRider problem statement

Rural children often walk more than an hour each way to school, and adult bicycles do not fit them while imported children's bikes break quickly and cannot be repaired locally. Design with, not for: requirements must come from co-design sessions and field trials with the intended users through a local partner.

## Users and context

_To be developed._

## Constraints

- Garage-buildable prototype, about $250 USD

## User research and co-design

This design is for communities the author is not part of, so requirements come from the people who will use it.

- [ ] Identify a local partner organization (Helpful Engineering network, NGO or university)
- [ ] Run co-design sessions with intended users; record who, where and what was learned
- [ ] Validate load, distance, terrain and cost assumptions in the field
- [ ] Revise requirements (REQ) from findings before freezing the design
